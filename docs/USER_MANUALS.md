# Per-Role User Manuals

## Customer / Patient
1. Open the frontend and choose Register or Log in.
2. Find a doctor using name or specialisation.
3. Select a session, review date/time/fee, and book.
4. Complete payment where required.
5. Watch the live queue on My Appointments.
6. When the doctor starts the consultation, open Consultation and chat.
7. Submit feedback after completion.

## Doctor
1. Log in and open Dashboard to see assigned sessions.
2. Start your session when scheduled.
3. Call the next patient; the queue and notification update live.
4. Mark arrival for physical patients where required.
5. Open the consultation room, chat, and record the consultation summary.
6. Complete or close the consultation.

## Manager
1. Log in and open Dashboard.
2. Create or edit sessions and assign doctors.
3. Use Reception to search appointments, record arrivals, and payments.
4. Create staff accounts; the specialisation dropdown loads from the API.
5. Publish notices and view reports.

## Administrator
1. Log in and open Staff Accounts.
2. Create or update staff users and reset passwords.
3. Manage rooms and specialisations where available.
4. Open Audit Logs to review login, consultation access, and administrative actions.
